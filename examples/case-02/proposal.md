# Diagnostic concept excerpt C2

This is a fictional concept excerpt. Review the access claim and its stated fallback only.

## P1. Question

How do practicing independent exhibition educators describe the reasons they give when selecting an example for a public workshop? The inference target is participants' reported reasoning, not observation of actual teaching or comparison of institutions.

## P2. Preferred access

A museum may circulate invitations to its freelance educators, but institutional permission is pending. The museum is a recruitment channel, not the object or required sampling unit.

## P3. Alternative route

A professional association has confirmed in writing that it can circulate the same opt-in invitation. Participants meet the same eligibility rule: at least three public workshops delivered in the past year. The study uses recorded voluntary interviews, subject to ethics approval and individual consent, with the same interview guide and planned thematic analysis. No institutional records, student data, or museum premises are needed.

## P4. Decision

If museum circulation is unavailable, recruitment will use the association. Neither route guarantees enrollment; the proposed initial target and stopping criteria still need specification in the full proposal.
