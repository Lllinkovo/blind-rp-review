# Diagnostic concept excerpt C1

This is a fictional concept excerpt. Review the stated causal claim and inference design only.

## P1. Question and intended contribution

Does receiving an audio guide, rather than a printed guide, cause visitors to remember more exhibition facts the following day? The central contribution is an estimate of the causal effect of guide format, distinct from differences between visitor groups.

## P2. Design

The study compares 30 members of an advanced art-history class visiting on Monday with 30 first-time visitors attending a public event on Saturday. The class receives audio guides; the public-event group receives printed guides. Text content is identical. No baseline knowledge, prior visits, or participant characteristics are collected. Assignment follows the two existing events and cannot be changed.

## P3. Analysis

The researcher will compare next-day mean quiz scores using a two-sample test. A statistically significant difference will establish the causal effect of audio rather than print. There is no other identifying evidence or planned adjustment.

## P4. Scope

A descriptive difference between these two groups would not answer the stated central question. The researcher has not proposed a design revision.
